## What is a surd?

A **surd** is an expression that includes a root, such as a square root or cube root.

Surds are often used to represent irrational numbers.

---

## Multiplying surds

When we multiply two surds together, it's the same thing as multiplying the numbers under the roots together.

In algebra:

$$
\sqrt{a}\times \sqrt{b}=\sqrt{ab}
$$
