# Ideal gas equation

For an [[ideal gas]]:

> $$
> pV=nRT
> $$

- $p$ is the [[pressure]] of the [[gas]], in [[pascal]]s (see [[gas pressure]]).
- $V$ is the [[volume]] of the [[gas]], in [[metre]]s cubed ($m^3$) (see [[gas volume]]).
- $T$ is the [[temperature]] of the [[gas]], in [[kelvin]].
- $n$ is the number of [[mole]]s in the [[gas]] (also see [[avogadro constant]]).
- $T$ is the [[gas constant]], in $J\,K^{-1}\,\text{mol}^{-1}$.
