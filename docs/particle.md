# Particle

A *particle* is anything which:
- Has negligible [[dimension]]s (doesn't take up any size).
- The direction of [[acceleration]] is the direction of the [[force]] applied (no spinning).
- Cannot be [[stretching|stretched]].
- ...Among some other assumptions.

In physics, we usually talk about particles as any sort of (usually small) [[matter]] or sometimes unit of [[energy]] (e.g. a [[photon]]).  
They're the *fundamental building blocks* of everything.

We can [[mechanics modelling assumptions|model mechanics problems as particles]], to simplify them.
