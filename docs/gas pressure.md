---
title: Gas pressure
---

A [[gas]] has a [[pressure]] because the [[particle]]s of the gas are moving and colliding with the walls of the container.

This exerts a [[force]] on the walls, which can be seen as [[pressure]].

- $\text{pressure}=\frac{force}{area}$, see [[pressure]].
- 

## Explaining gas pressure

- [[gas]] [[particle]]s collide with the wall of a container.
- The particle changes [[momentum]] (its magnitude is $2mv$).
- Upon collision, there are *equal and opposite forces* on the wall and the particle.
