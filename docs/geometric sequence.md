---
title: Geometric sequence
---

## Term-to-term rule

We write the *term* to term rule ([[inductive sequence definition]]) as:

> - $u_{n+1}=ru_n$
> - $u_1=a$

$r$ is the multiplier that the terms increase by; $a$ is the initial term.

## Position-to-term rule

The *position* to term rule ([[deductive sequence definition]]) for a geometric sequence is:

> $u_n=a\times r^{n-1}$

## Ratio between terms

The *ratios* of two consecutive terms in a geometric sequence are always equal.

That means for a sequence $x,\,y,\,z,\,...$, $\frac yx=\frac zy$
