---
title: Normal distribution
---

- 68.2% of data lies within *one [[standard deviation]]* of the mean.

$$
Z=\frac{x-\mu}\sigma
$$

- $Z$ is the number of standard deviations from the [[mean]].
- $x$ is a single point of data.
- $\mu$ is the *accepted* population mean.
- $\sigma$ is the standard deviation.
