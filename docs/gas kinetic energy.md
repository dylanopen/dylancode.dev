---
title: Gas kinetic energy
---

In a gas:

> $$
> \frac12m(c_{\text{rms}})^2=\frac32kT
> $$

- $m$ is the mass of gas.
- $c_{rms}$ is the [[root mean square average]] of the [[kinetic energy]] of the [[gas]] [[particle]]s.
- $k$ is the [[boltzmann constant]].
- $T$ is the temperature of the [[gas]], in [[kelvin]].
