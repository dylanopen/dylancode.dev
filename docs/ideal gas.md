---
title: Ideal gas
---

In an **ideal [[gas]]**:

- All collisions between [[atom]]s or [[molecule]]s are perfectly [[elastic collision|elastic]].
- There are no intermolecular attractive forces
- The gas [[particle]]s do not collide with *each other*.
- There is no [[potential energy]] stored in the particles.
	- So the [[internal energy]] is equal to the [[kinetic energy]] (all internal energy is kinetic).

## Equation

The [[ideal gas equation]] can be used to calculate things about an ideal gas.
