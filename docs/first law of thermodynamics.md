# First law of thermodynamics

> The change in internal energy is equal to the sum of energy transfers through heating and work done.

## Equation

We can write that statement as:

> $$
> \Delta U=\Delta Q+\Delta W
> $$

- $\Delta U$ is the change in [[internal energy]]
- $\Delta Q$ is the amount of [[thermal energy]] added
- $\Delta W$ is the amount of [[work done]]

All units are in [[joule]]s ($J$).
