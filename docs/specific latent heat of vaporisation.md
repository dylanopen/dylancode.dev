# Specific latent heat of vaporisation

The [[specific latent heat]] of [[vaporisation]] is the amount of [[thermal energy]] required to evaporate $1kg$ a [[liquid]] into a [[gas]].



## [[flashcards]]

| Question                                          | Answer                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What is the specific latent heat of vaporisation? | The amount of thermal energy required to boil $1kg$ of a liquid into a gas. |
